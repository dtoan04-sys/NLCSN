## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene, prestigious ambiance of an ultra-premium botanical atelier. Designed for discerning collectors and passionate indoor horticulturists seeking rare aroids—such as variegated Monsteras, velvety Anthuriums, and sculptural Philodendrons—the experience prioritizes curatorial dignity over transactional clutter.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Botanical Minimalist Luxury
The visual identity draws from high-end gallery design and contemporary European botanical monographs:
- **Pristine Negative Space:** Expansive breathing room frames each specimen like a living sculpture.
- **Architectural Subtlety:** Hairline division, translucent moss-tinted borders, and muted surfaces avoid distracting from variegated leaf patterns and subtle foliage textures.
- **Refined Typographic Cadence:** Ultra-clean geometric letterforms paired with slanted editorial botanical nomenclature elevate provenance, clone types, and cultivar specifications.
- **Bilingual Nuance:** Seamless Vietnamese botanical naming and collection tags alongside Latin taxonomy ensure both native fluency and international botanical authority.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the lighting gradient of an old-growth greenhouse canopy, relying on deep chlorophyll shadows, milky emergent growth accents, and porcelain-toned backgrounds.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Primary Canvas (`#F7F9F6`):** An airy, muted ivory with faint undertones of damp stone. Used across root page backgrounds to reduce harsh digital glare.
- **Secondary Canvas / Elevated Surface (`#F0F4F1`):** A subtle cool-sage ground layer for table headers, secondary modular sections, and inactive filter pills.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used exclusively on product containers, modals, and inspection panels to maximize specimen color fidelity.
- **Primary Core (`#1B4332`):** Saturated deep evergreen used for essential action buttons, high-emphasis text, primary headers, and brand marks.
- **Primary Deep (`#081C15`):** Black-forest tone reserved for maximum typography contrast, high-tier status badges, and footer blocks.
- **Accent Vibrant (`#52B788`):** Mid-tone sage used for active selectors, cart badges, and focal interactive states.
- **Accent Soft (`#74C69D` & `#95D5B2`):** Soft foliage greens for iconography, secondary button hover states, and stock availability indicators.
- **Accent Mist (`#D8F3DC`):** Tender sprout tint for chip backgrounds, positive humidity indicators, and voucher banners.
- **Hairline Border (`#E3E8E4`):** Soft organic border tone engineered for high-DPI displays to provide structure without heavy visual boundaries.

## Typography

Typography balances geometric precision with botanical conventions. Clean weights deliver a contemporary retail presence, while specific typographic stylings handle scientific taxonomies and Vietnamese diacritics.

### Hierarchy & Botanical Convention
- **Taxonomic Styling (`scientific-name`):** Always render binomial botanical nomenclature (*Genus species*, cultivar, or forma) in `font-style: italic` with balanced kerning. This sets cultivar identifiers (e.g., *Philodendron spiritus-sancti*, *Monstera deliciosa aurea*) apart from common names and localized titles.
- **Vietnamese Diacritic Legibility:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` is tuned to ensure Vietnamese tonal marks (dấu hỏi, dấu ngã, dấu nặng) maintain vertical rhythm without clipping or crowding upper-tier headings.
- **Weight Pairing:** Editorial headlines favor light and regular weights (`300` / `400`) at scale to maintain an airy boutique feel, reserving bold weights (`600` / `700`) for structural navigation, price data, and metadata chips.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column grid system prioritizing product photography, specimen provenance, and high-resolution foliage inspections.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Plant cards render single-column or twin-column staggered galleries with horizontal touch-scrolling chips.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column structure with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Specimen grids shift to 2-3 column configurations.
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column structure with `margin-desktop: 5rem` and maximum layout containment capped at 1440px. Gutters expand to `2.5rem` to produce an editorial magazine composition.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering, ultra-soft green-tinted shadows, and delicate hairline boundaries rather than high-contrast drops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F7F9F6` base page canvas with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Specimen Cards & Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` crisp white card surface bounded by a subtle `1px solid #E3E8E4` border. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an organic diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Inspection Modals & Sticky Cart Panel):** Glass-influenced modal backdrop (`rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) paired with foreground containers elevated via `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The system implements a disciplined, architectural shape geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Soft 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding provides gentle organic tactile touchpoints while retaining precise, luxury-oriented structure.

### Corner Radii
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Form inputs, buttons, standard card containers, image viewports, and system checkboxes.
- **Medium Panels (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / `8px`):** Flyout drawers, checkout containers, and gallery lightboxes.
- **Capsule / Pill (`rounded-full`):** Category pills, certification tags (e.g., "Mô bản gốc", "Cây thuần chậu"), and quick-action floaters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Sở hữu ngay" / "Thêm vào giỏ"):** `#1B4332` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` corner radius, `font-weight: 600`, letter-spaced `0.04em`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: shifts to `#081C15` with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary / Ghost ("Xem chi tiết cá thể"):** Border `1px solid #1B4332`, transparent background, `#1B4332` text. Hover: `#F0F4F1` subtle tint fill.
- **Tertiary Accent:** `#D8F3DC` background with `#1B4332` text for utility cart interactions and specimen filter resetting.

### Chips & Botanical Tags
- **Cultivar Identification Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`, text `label-md`. Background `#F0F4F1`, text `#1B4332`, border `1px solid #E3E8E4`.
- **Rare Specimen Flag:** Background `#D8F3DC`, text `#081C15`, border `1px solid #95D5B2`. Indicator dot `6px` tinted `#52B788`.
- **Vietnamese Status Tags:** Examples like *"Đã thuần dưỡng"* (Acclimated) or *"Cắt ngọn trực tiếp"* (Top cutting) rendered in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Cards (Product & Specimen Profile)
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E3E8E4` outline.
- **Media Canvas:** Aspect ratio `4:5` displaying high-resolution specimen photography with neutral off-white backing (`#F7F9F6`).
- **Metadata Stack:**
  - Common localized name in `headline-sm` (`#081C15`).
  - Taxonomic scientific name in `scientific-name` italic (`#1B4332`).
  - Specimen attributes: leaf count, root state, variegation tier.
  - Price rendered in `headline-md` tabular figures with currency indicator (`₫`).

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, `1px solid #E3E8E4` border, `12px 16px` padding, `body-md` text. Focus state transitions to `1px solid #1B4332` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(116, 198, 157, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` squares/circles with `1px solid #95D5B2`. Selected state fills with `#1B4332` containing a clean white geometric checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Specimen Passport (Hộ chiếu cây):** Dedicated certification block detailing mother plant DNA, node count, acclimatization age, and light/humidity requirements.
- **Variegation Grade Meter:** Segmented visual bar in `#52B788` tracking marble, sectoral, or half-moon leaf coverage percentages.
