## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the organic vitality of modern indoor horticulture with the sleek precision of artificial intelligence. Crafted specifically for an intelligent plant commerce and diagnosis platform, the interface balances natural serenity with smart technological clarity.

The target audience spans urban plant enthusiasts, interior decor hobbyists, and commercial botanists who seek curated green aesthetics alongside instant, reliable AI diagnostics. The aesthetic blends **Modern Organic Minimalism** with subtle **Bioluminescent Glassmorphism** reserved for computational intelligence features. 

Interactions feel breathing, tactile, and peaceful. Surfaces are bright, crisp, and airy, evoking morning light filtering through lush canopy foliage, punctuated by precise computational accents that signal cutting-edge computer vision.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from living flora, fertile soils, and high-precision sensor vision:

- **Primary Forest Series (`#2E7D32` base, `#1B5E20` deep, `#4CAF50` vibrant):** Anchors foundational interactive touchpoints, primary actions, and brand identification. Reflects chlorophyllic vitality and reliability.
- **Secondary Sage Series (`#81C784` accent, `#C8E6C9` wash, `#E8F5E9` container):** Serves as soft foundational fills, categorized tag backgrounds, and low-cognitive-load hover states.
- **Warm Accents (`#D87D4A` Terracotta & `#F59E0B` Amber):** Represents sun-baked earthen pots and warm illumination. Strictly reserved for transactional urgency, promotional badges, customer rating stars, and health warning flags.
- **Botanical Neutrals (`#FFFFFF` surface, `#F9FBF9` canvas base, `#F1F5F2` container-low, `#223326` ink):** A chlorophyll-tinted grayscale that replaces sterile slate/black with deep botanical moss tones for text, ensuring reading comfort and organic unity.
- **AI Recognition Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #06B6D4 100%)`):** A specialized bioluminescent gradient reserved exclusively for the AI camera scanner, identification overlays, confidence score trackers, and automated diagnostic badges.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its tall x-height, open apertures, and geometric yet soft curves render diacritical marks in Vietnamese with utmost legibility, balance, and spatial elegance.

Headlines employ tighter letter-spacing and substantial weights (`700`, `800`) to anchor plant taxonomic titles with architectural poise. Body typography uses generous line heights (1.55 to 1.65) to maximize readability across care guides, soil compositions, and diagnostic symptom logs. Labels, technical parameters (e.g., quang hợp, độ ẩm, ánh sáng), and badges utilize slightly tracked, medium-to-bold weights for rapid scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure leverages a fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet devices, and a focused 4-column layout on mobile devices. 

Margins and gutters contract intentionally on smaller viewports (`1rem`) to provide high visual density for product galleries, while expanding generously on desktop (`2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters) to maintain an expansive botanical journal feel. 

Spacing rhythm is strictly governed by an 8px base unit (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Component interiors feature generous airiness, giving plant photography room to breathe without competing against structural borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering** coupled with **Plant-Tinted Ambient Shadows** and **Bioluminescent Shimmers**:

- **Surface Base:** `#F9FBF9` provides an organic, non-glare foundation.
- **Card and Container Tier:** `#FFFFFF` resting elevated above the canvas using a soft, organic drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(27, 94, 32, 0.12)`, offering lift without stark charcoal shadows.
- **AI Scanning HUD & Recognition Overlays:** Semi-transparent glassmorphic frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) bordered by an ethereal luminescent cyan/emerald perimeter stroke (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`) and an outward cyan glow `0 0 24px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- **Ghost Borders:** Non-elevated containers utilize subtle organic outlines composed of `#E2EAE3` instead of generic gray boundaries.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy mirrors natural botanical curvatures: succulent leaves, smooth river pebbles, and curved planters. 

Interactive controls, buttons, and inputs utilize `rounded-xl` (12px to 16px) for an inviting, tactile grasp. Product cards, botanical case studies, and AI scanning viewports apply generous `rounded-2xl` (20px to 24px) corners to frame images organically. Micro elements such as badges, status chips, and avatar containers use complete pill radii (`rounded-full`) to complement the flowing layout.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Mua ngay, Thêm vào giỏ):** Solid `#2E7D32` background with white text, `rounded-xl` geometry, `space-md` horizontal padding, and subtle elevation. Hover transitions to `#1B5E20` with a soft green glow.
- **Secondary Action (Xem chi tiết, Hướng dẫn chăm sóc):** `#E8F5E9` background with `#2E7D32` text, zero border, transitioning to `#C8E6C9` on interaction.
- **AI Feature Button (Nhận diện cây AI):** High-impact bioluminescent gradient fill (`#10B981` to `#06B6D4`), crisp white text, accompanied by a dynamic sparkle icon. Hover amplifies outer luminescence.

### Product & Diagnostic Cards
- **Product Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `rounded-2xl` radius, cushioned with `space-md` padding. Image frames showcase high-resolution photography with rounded interior corners (`rounded-xl`). Price uses deep `#1B5E20`, while promotional badges float at top-left.
- **AI Plant Health Diagnostic Card:** Dual-state card. Displays an upper scanning viewport with dynamic targeting reticles, flowing into lower health metrics: Độ ẩm đất (Soil Moisture), Ánh sáng (Light Intake), and Sức khỏe lá (Foliage Health) expressed as progress arcs.

### Badges & Chips
- **Botanical Badges:** Filter tags (`Trong nhà`, `Dễ chăm sóc`, `Lọc không khí`) use `#E8F5E9` base with `#2E7D32` text and full pill radii (`rounded-full`).
- **Urgency & Discount Badges:** Warm Terracotta `#D87D4A` fill with white typography for seasonal sales (`-25% Mùa Xuân`).
- **Rating Badges:** Sunshine Amber `#F59E0B` icon with `#223326` value display (`5.0 ★`).
- **AI Confidence Score Chip:** Frosted glass backing with cyan-mint border and glowing dot indicator (`Độ chính xác 98.4%`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields (Tìm kiếm giống cây, Nhập địa chỉ):** Surface of `#FFFFFF` with `#E2EAE3` border, `rounded-xl` curvature, and deep botanical ink typography. Focus state replaces gray stroke with `#2E7D32` and a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.15)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic checks (`rounded-md` and `rounded-full`), filling with `#2E7D32` upon selection, surrounded by a gentle sage halo on focus.

### Lists & Care Steps
- **Care Guide Checklist:** Horizontal step-cards featuring circular step markers numbered in Terracotta or Forest Green, paired with subtle dotted dividers evoking garden trellis patterns.
